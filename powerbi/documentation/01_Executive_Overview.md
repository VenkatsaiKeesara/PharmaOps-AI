# 📊 Executive Overview

## Dashboard Name

Executive Overview

---

# Objective

The Executive Overview dashboard provides a high-level summary of the pharmacy's business performance. It enables executives and decision-makers to monitor revenue, profitability, inventory investment, operational risks, and inventory health from a single dashboard.

---

# Target Audience

- CEO
- Pharmacy Director
- Operations Manager
- Business Analyst
- Inventory Manager

---

# Business Problem

Pharmacy executives often lack a centralized dashboard that combines financial performance, inventory efficiency, and operational risks into a single view. Decision-makers spend significant time analyzing multiple reports instead of focusing on strategic actions.

This dashboard solves that problem by presenting key business metrics in a unified executive view.

---

# Business Questions

1. What is the total revenue generated by the pharmacy?
2. How much gross profit has been generated?
3. What is the total inventory investment?
4. How much money has been lost due to medicine waste?
5. What is the average profit margin?
6. How efficiently is inventory generating returns?
7. What is the current executive risk level?
8. What is the overall inventory health score?

---

# KPIs Implemented

| KPI | Business Purpose |
|------|------------------|
| KPI_Total Revenue | Calculates total business revenue. |
| KPI_Total Gross Profit | Calculates total potential gross profit. |
| KPI_Total Inventory Value | Calculates the total value of inventory currently held. |
| KPI_Total Waste Value | Calculates the financial impact of medicine waste. |
| KPI_Average Profit Margin | Measures average business profitability. |
| KPI_Average Inventory ROI | Measures return generated from inventory investment. |
| KPI_Average Executive Risk Score | Indicates the overall operational risk level. |
| KPI_Average Inventory Health Score | Measures the overall health of the pharmacy inventory. |

---

# DAX Measures Created

| Measure | Aggregation |
