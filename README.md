💊 PharmaOps-AI

AI-Powered Pharmacy Inventory & Drug Waste Optimization Platform

PharmaOps-AI is an enterprise-scale end-to-end healthcare analytics platform developed to optimize pharmacy inventory management, minimize medicine waste, improve procurement efficiency, and support executive decision-making through data-driven insights.

The solution combines data engineering, exploratory data analysis, feature engineering, business analytics, dimensional modeling, Power BI dashboard development, and an explainable AI recommendation engine into a unified analytics pipeline.

❗ Problem Statement

Modern pharmacies face challenges including inventory imbalance, medicine expiry, supplier performance monitoring, procurement inefficiencies, and limited executive-level insights.

PharmaOps-AI transforms raw healthcare data into actionable business intelligence.
